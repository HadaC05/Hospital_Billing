Terms and Conditions
1. Consent to Treatment

I hereby authorize the hospital and its medical staff to provide necessary medical treatment, care, and services deemed advisable by the attending physician. This consent includes, but is not limited to, diagnostic procedures, medical treatments, surgical procedures, and anesthesia.

2. Financial Responsibility

I understand that I am financially responsible for all charges incurred for my treatment, care, and services provided by the hospital. This includes charges for physicians, surgeons, anesthesiologists, radiologists, pathologists, and other professional services, as well as charges for hospital accommodations, medications, supplies, and equipment.

3. Release of Medical Information

I consent to the release of my medical information to insurance companies, healthcare providers, and other third parties as necessary for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations. I understand that this information will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

4. Hospital Policies

I agree to abide by all hospital policies and regulations, including those related to patient conduct, visitation, and property. I understand that failure to comply with these policies may result in discharge from the hospital.

5. Discharge Planning

I understand that the hospital will begin discharge planning upon admission and that I will be expected to participate in this process. I agree to make arrangements for my discharge as recommended by my healthcare team.

6. Acknowledgment

I acknowledge that I have read and understood these terms and conditions, and I agree to be bound by them. I understand that this consent is valid for the duration of my hospital stay unless revoked in writing.
